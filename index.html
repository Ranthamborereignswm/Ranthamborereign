Planning a Ranthambore Safari

Ranthambore National Park in Rajasthan is known for its forest landscapes and wildlife. If you are planning a tiger safari, share your travel date, number of visitors and preferred morning or afternoon slot with Ranthambore Reign.

Safari enquiry and availability: Safari zones, vehicle options, permits, timings and prices can vary. Send an enquiry to ask for current details, and verify final availability and booking arrangements before paying or travelling.

How to enquire: Use the form on this page to prepare a WhatsApp message. Choose either contact number, review the details, and press Send in WhatsApp. Your enquiry is not a confirmed safari reservation, and wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Useful planning details: Keep your preferred date, visitor count and contact number ready. Follow official park instructions and respect wildlife and forest rules during your visit.

Before you head out

Good to know.

A few quick answers before your journey begins.

Will my details reach WhatsApp automatically?

When you submit the form, WhatsApp opens with your details filled into a message. You must tap Send in WhatsApp for the message to reach the selected number. This avoids sending anything without your action.

Can I contact either WhatsApp number?

Yes. Choose one of the two WhatsApp numbers in the form. If you need to contact the other number as well, open its WhatsApp link in the contact section and send the enquiry there separately.

Does an enquiry confirm my safari?

No. Your enquiry is not a booking confirmation. Safari permits, timings, zone availability and final arrangements must be verified before travel.

Can you guarantee a tiger sighting?

No. Ranthambore is a wild habitat, and sightings can never be guaranteed. Please enjoy the forest responsibly and follow your guide and park rules.

How much does a safari cost?

Costs can vary by date, safari type, availability and applicable official fees. Send your preferred date and group size on WhatsApp to ask for current details before making a payment.

When should I send my enquiry?

As early as you can, especially if your travel dates are fixed. Availability is not guaranteed until it has been checked and confirmed through the appropriate booking process.
